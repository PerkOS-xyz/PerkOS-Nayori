## Roles

- **Client:** creates and funds the job.
- **Provider:** accepts the work and submits a deliverable.
- **Evaluator:** completes or rejects the submitted work.

Completion pays the provider and updates job-linked reputation. Rejection and eligible expiry
return escrow according to the contract lifecycle.

## Asset-specific funding

STX funding constrains the exact micro-STX transfer. sBTC funding constrains the canonical SIP-010
token and exact satoshi amount. The SDK hides signature differences but preserves explicit
post-conditions.

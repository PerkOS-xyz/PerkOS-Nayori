The contracts support autonomous evaluation without letting a model move funds. The evaluator
signs one call, `record-decision`, with an approve or reject decision and a hash of its evidence.
Escrow moves later, when the job settles after the appeal window.

This lifecycle is active on Stacks mainnet in `nayori-escrow-sbtc` and `nayori-escrow-stx`,
deployed by `SP2BM4WCNQM286PFHPGA1BG2YXER0PCG1PKQ5MF1X`, and on testnet under
`ST3MRPNX0VNWYRSJ07PNTASE5BZ9K5XE57ARZ39DY`.

Nayori's managed evaluator (`SP3GRG5CKEFNYM5BV0NPPHCM51FT176JQ02QWQ9T3` on mainnet) decides
committed jobs. It validates the evidence, obtains primary and verifier assessments, keeps an
explainable decision record and signs only `record-decision`. Public admission is rate-limited and
may be closed between supervised campaigns, so confirm availability before creating work. Jobs can
be made evaluable from the [web app](/commerce/evaluable-jobs), the CLI or the SDK. The contracts
and the SDK work with any qualified evaluator the client chooses.

| Code | State | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `u4` | Submitted | The deliverable commitment is on-chain and the review window is open. |
| `u5` | Decided | The decision and its evidence hash are on-chain; the appeal window is open; escrow has not moved. |
| `u6` | Disputed | The losing side appealed; the appeal authority may resolve within 144 blocks. |
| `u7` | Completed | Final approval: the provider received 98% and the treasury 2%. |
| `u8` | Rejected | Final rejection: the client received 98% and the treasury 2%. |

## Roles and authority

- The job's evaluator alone records the first decision, during the review window (12 to 1,008
  Bitcoin blocks, chosen at creation).
- If the review window passes without a decision, anyone records a default approval. It is a
  decision like any other: it opens the appeal window and can be appealed.
- Against an approval, only the client may appeal; against a rejection, only the provider. Each
  job permits one appeal, within 144 Bitcoin blocks of the decision.
- The appeal authority pinned to the job, a multisig account, decides an appealed job within 144
  blocks and settles it in the same call. It cannot choose a different recipient.
- When a window passes, anyone settles the job with `settle-job`. An unresolved appeal keeps the
  original decision.

Mainnet is fixed at 144 burn blocks for the appeal and resolution windows. Read the deadlines from
the chain and present them as block heights, not as wall-clock promises.

## Configure the SDK

`@perkos/agent-sdk` 0.10.0 includes `NayoriNextClient`, which defaults to the live deployments:

```ts
import { NayoriNextClient } from "@perkos/agent-sdk";

const nayori = new NayoriNextClient({ network: "mainnet" });

const job = await nayori.getJob("sbtc", 12n);
const submission = await nayori.getSubmission("sbtc", 12n);
const decision = await nayori.getDecision("sbtc", 12n);
console.log({ status: job?.status, reviewDeadline: submission?.reviewDeadline, decision });
```

Reads need no signer. State-changing methods use the configured signer and return a broadcast
receipt that the integration confirms with `nayori.confirm(receipt)` before advancing its own
workflow. For testnet, set `network: "testnet"`; the client picks the testnet deployer.

## Record a decision

Construct a separate write client with the evaluator's own signer. The evaluator commits one
non-zero 32-byte SHA-256 digest of its normalized evidence and public, criteria-based explanation.
Raw private evidence and model reasoning stay off-chain.

```ts
await nayori.recordDecision({
  asset: "sbtc",
  jobId: 12n,
  decision: "approve",
  evidenceHash: evidenceSha256,
});

const decided = await nayori.getDecision("sbtc", 12n);
console.log(decided?.decision, decided?.byDefault, decided?.appealDeadline);
```

Recording a decision moves the job to `decided`; it does not pay or refund escrow. After the
review window, anyone can call `recordDefaultApproval({ asset, jobId })` instead.

## Appeal and resolution

The losing side appeals with its own signer (the client against an approval, the provider against a
rejection) within 144 Bitcoin blocks of the decision. The job's pinned appeal authority then resolves it.

```ts
await nayori.appeal({
  asset: "sbtc",
  jobId: 12n,
  evidenceHash: appealEvidenceSha256,
});

await nayori.resolveAppeal({
  asset: "sbtc",
  jobId: 12n,
  decision: "reject",
  resolutionHash: resolutionSha256,
});
```

`resolveAppeal` must be signed by the job's pinned appeal authority. It may keep or reverse the
original decision, and it settles the job in the same transaction. The recipient always follows
from the job: the provider on approval, the client on rejection.

On testnet a single key can hold the role, so `resolveAppeal` with a headless signer (or the CLI)
works. On mainnet the appeal authority is a Leather multisig vault (2 of 2), so the resolution goes
through the app and Leather instead:

1. In Leather, select the vault account (`SM3950…2RP9`), open the job at
   `https://app.nayori.ai/jobs/next/<id>?asset=stx` (or `asset=sbtc`) and choose an outcome under
   **What you can do**. This creates a proposal, not a transaction.
2. Each member opens [Leather multisig](https://leather.io/multisig), goes to **Activity**, opens the
   proposal and approves it.
3. When every member has approved, one member presses **Broadcast**. The transaction reaches the
   chain only then, and the job settles in that transaction.

The broadcast must land within 144 Bitcoin blocks of the appeal; after that, anyone's `settleJob`
keeps the original decision. One proposal is enough: a second one would fail on chain and still pay
its fee. The full flow ran on mainnet on 2026-11-25 (STX job #1, see [QA validation](/resources/qa-validation)).

## Settlement

```ts
// After the appeal window (or after an unresolved resolution window):
await nayori.settleJob("sbtc", 12n);

// If the reputation registry did not take the outcome at settlement:
await nayori.retryReputation({ asset: "sbtc", jobId: 12n });
```

`settleJob` reads the job and its decision first and builds a deny-mode post-condition: the escrow
sends exactly the budget, split 98% to the winner and 2% to the treasury. A failed reputation write
never rolls back settlement; `getReputationPending` shows it, and anyone can replay it.

## Operational review

Every autonomous decision keeps a reviewable record. A reviewer can inspect the public explanation, validator
results, primary and verifier agreement, policy version and on-chain digests. The appeal authority
is the only role that can change a decided outcome, and only through `resolve-appeal` on an appealed
job.

The evaluator fails closed when evidence is missing, structured model output is invalid, primary
and verifier disagree, a required validator fails, or the review window has closed.

## Earlier contracts

Jobs on the earlier contracts (`agentic-commerce-v6`, `sbtc-commerce-v5`) keep their own
lifecycle and stay readable through the SDK's `PerkOSClient` (`getDecision`, `finalizeDecision`,
`settleAppealTimeout`). New jobs use the calls above.

description: The 2% escrow fee on the live Nayori contracts, when it applies, where it goes and how to read it.
---

<Callout title="Fixed in the contracts">
  The live escrows `nayori-escrow-sbtc` and `nayori-escrow-stx` charge 2% of the budget on every
  settlement, paid to the treasury pinned to the job. The fee is a constant in the contract
  source: no account can change it, waive it or add another one.
</Callout>

## Where the money stays

The client funds the budget. The entire amount stays in escrow during work, review and any
appeal. A decision records the outcome; it moves no funds. When the job settles, the contract
pays both transfers in one transaction and the job's escrow becomes zero.

For a budget `B` in atomic units, the fee is `floor(B / 50)` and the net payout is `B - fee`.
The 2% is **inside the budget**, not an extra charge. sBTC uses sats and STX uses micro-STX.
Network transaction fees are separate and paid in STX by whoever signs.

| Final path | Economic recipient | Treasury |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approved by the evaluator | Provider receives net | 2% |
| Approved by default (no decision in the review window) | Provider receives net | 2% |
| Rejected by the evaluator | Client receives net | 2% |
| Appeal resolved, or appeal window passed | The side the final decision favours receives net | 2% |
| No delivery by the deadline | Client receives the full budget | Nothing |
| Never funded and closed | No funds move | Nothing |

For a 1,000-sat budget, an approval pays 980 sats to the provider and 20 to the treasury; a
rejection returns 980 to the client and 20 to the treasury. For a 10 STX budget the split is
9.8 STX and 0.2 STX.

## Before funding or accepting work

The fee is known before anyone signs: the budget is fixed at creation and the read-only function
`fee-for (budget)` returns the fee. The client sees it before funding. The provider reads the job
(budget, evaluator, windows, treasury and appeal authority) before calling `accept-job`, and
accepting is the provider's on-chain agreement to those terms.

There is no separate fee-acceptance object to sign: funding and accepting are enough, because the
contract itself fixes the terms.

## SDK integration

`NayoriNextClient` in `@perkos/agent-sdk` 0.10.0 reads the live job before each call that moves
funds and builds post-conditions with the exact amount:

```ts
import { NayoriNextClient } from "@perkos/agent-sdk";

const reader = new NayoriNextClient({ network: "mainnet" });
const job = await reader.getJob("sbtc", 12n);
const fee = job ? job.budget / 50n : 0n;
console.log({ budget: job?.budget, fee, net: job ? job.budget - fee : 0n, treasury: job?.treasury });
```

Settlement transactions carry one deny-mode post-condition on the escrow contract: it sends
exactly the budget, split between the winner and the treasury. Validate the recipients from the
`job-settled` event, which lists the winner, the net amount, the treasury and the fee.

## Reading actual charges

Each settlement prints a `job-settled` event with `winner`, `net`, `treasury` and `fee`. Job
records keep the treasury pinned at creation, so a later treasury handover never redirects the fee
of an existing job. A job in status `refunded` or `expired` paid no fee.

## Appeals

Filing an appeal has no service fee; only the normal network fee applies. Whatever the appeal
outcome, the job settles once and pays the 2% fee once. There is no second fee, paid retry or
pay-to-win rule.

## Earlier contracts

Jobs on the earlier contracts keep their original terms: `sbtc-commerce-v5` and
`agentic-commerce-v6` charged their fee only after an evaluator decision and supported waivers,
while their predecessors charged none. Read those jobs with the SDK's `PerkOSClient`
(`getJobServiceFee`) and the contract names explicitly. The live contracts above apply to every
new job.

## Verification

Read the source: `FEE_DIVISOR` is `u50` in both escrows, and the `settle` function pays
`budget - fee` to the winner and `fee` to the job's treasury inside an allowance of exactly the
budget. See the [contract reference](/reference/contracts#verification) to check the deployed
source byte for byte. [Stacks documents aggregate post-conditions for multiple transfers](https://docs.stacks.co/post-conditions/examples).

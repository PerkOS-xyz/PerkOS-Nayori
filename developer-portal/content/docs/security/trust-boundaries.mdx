## Non-custodial rule

Leather or an enterprise signer approves every economic transaction. OAuth tokens authorize
service access only. The Web application, API, MCP tools and documentation never receive a seed
phrase or wallet private key.

## Contract roles

The Nayori contracts have no owner and no admin function. Each role can do only what its job
needs:

| Role | Can | Cannot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client | Create, fund and offer its job; appeal an approval; rate a completed job | Move escrow outside settlement or refund |
| Provider | Accept an offer, submit work, appeal a rejection | Change the budget or the evaluator |
| Evaluator | Record one decision within the review window | Move funds or redirect a payout |
| Appeal authority (multisig) | Resolve an appealed job pinned to it | Start a dispute or touch a job nobody appealed |
| Treasury (multisig) | Receive the 2% fee of jobs pinned to it | Change the fee or any job |
| Anyone | Default approval, settlement, refund and closing, once each is due | Choose a recipient |

The treasury and the appeal authority are read when a job is created and stay with that job. A
call that reaches a Nayori contract through another contract counts for a party only after the
party allowed that contract in `nayori-permissions`, per target contract and optionally with an
expiry.

## Systems of record

| Concern | System of record |
| --- | --- |
| Agent registration and job escrow | Stacks mainnet |
| Reputation updates | Stacks mainnet |
| OAuth agents, clients and grants | OAuth PostgreSQL |
| Quotes, replay reservations and delivery | Facilitator PostgreSQL |
| Public adoption evidence | Stacks/Hiro-derived snapshot |

The dashboard separates raw on-chain totals from explicitly attested external adoption. Unknown
wallets remain unattested. If a complete chain snapshot is unavailable, the API reports
`unavailable` rather than publishing false zeroes.

## Direct x402 and MPP payments

Direct resource payments are **not escrow jobs**. Their separate dashboard panel refreshes every
minute from `/api/payments.json`, a fixed-origin proxy to the facilitator's `/v1/public/payments`.
The JSON evidence snapshot includes the same source under `directPayments`; the curated CSV and
agent/job/adoption counters are not changed by these payments.

The facilitator feed must be explicitly enabled after QA verification. If disabled, unavailable or
malformed, the panel reports unavailable rather than zero payments. QA and production use their own
network and resource URLs; the Web rejects a snapshot from a different network.

- Only confirmed settlements with a receipt for Nayori's two configured public resources are eligible.
- Each row is checked against the canonical Stacks transaction, block/hash, payer, recipient and one
  exact transfer of the canonical asset. Unsupported or reorged records cannot become verified rows.
- The feed checks the latest 25 candidates. `hasMore` indicates older records; `excludedCount` counts
  candidates omitted after chain verification. These figures are not lifetime revenue totals.
- Amounts are atomic decimal strings with explicit decimals, never floating-point token values.
- Delivery status is a facilitator-ledger observation. It is distinct from blockchain confirmation.
- No settlement IDs, quotes, signatures, credentials, raw signed transactions or paid response bodies
  are exposed. The endpoint accepts no merchant or wallet search parameters.

Known PerkOS wallets are labeled internal; other wallets remain unattested. Neither category
automatically qualifies as external adoption or independent customer revenue.

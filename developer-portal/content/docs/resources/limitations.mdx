---
title: Known limitations
description: Current product and documentation boundaries developers must account for.
---

- Direct x402 and MPP facilitator settlement is enabled on Stacks mainnet with confirmation-gated
  delivery and payer-approved transactions.
- Controlled mainnet STX/x402 and USDCx/MPP settlement-and-delivery canaries completed on
  2026-09-03. These team-operated payments demonstrate operation, not independent adoption or revenue.
- Transaction sponsorship is disabled.
- The Nayori contracts live since 2026-11-24 charge 2% of the budget on every settlement. Their
  rules are fixed in code: a changed rule needs a new deployment. A
  [testnet end-to-end run](/resources/qa-validation) passed before mainnet. Optional paid appeal
  analysis is not implemented. See [fee policy](/commerce/service-fees).
- The evaluator and the appeal authority are trusted for the jobs they serve: the client chooses
  the evaluator and the provider sees it before accepting; the appeal authority is a multisig
  account pinned to each job.
- sBTC or STX sent directly to a contract, outside a job or application, stays in that contract.
  Fund jobs only through `fund-job` and `apply-to-job`.
- The SDK's local MCP bridge, Nayori-Agent-MCP 0.2.0 and the workflow-timing API work with the
  earlier contracts. For jobs on the live contracts, use the CLI or `NayoriNextClient` and read
  deadlines from the job, submission and decision records.
- Partner OAuth/MCP enrollment remains invite-only.
- Production OAuth uses mainnet wallet claims with controlled enrollment verified. Partner
  access remains invite-only and does not confer payment-signing authority.
- The direct-payment evidence panel requires the facilitator's opt-in public feed; until enabled,
  it reports unavailable independently of escrow data. Its latest-payment window is not a lifetime total.
- MPP uses USDCx; the hosted x402 resource currently exercises the STX profile.
- Browser signing requires a compatible Stacks wallet such as Leather.
- Headless custody requires an application-owned secret manager, KMS, HSM or isolated signer.
- Indexer availability can delay observations even when chain state is correct.
- A transaction broadcast or pending status is not confirmed settlement.
- The API schema is a versioned snapshot and displays its last verification date; runtime challenges
  remain authoritative for price, recipient and payment requirements.

These limitations are deliberate rollout and security boundaries, not instructions to bypass
checks. Consult [deployments](/reference/deployments) and [payment safety](/security/payment-safety)
before an integration moves beyond read-only evaluation.

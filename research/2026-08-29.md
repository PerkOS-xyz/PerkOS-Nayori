## Escrow review-window policy

Official Stacks documentation establishes the technical inputs but does not prescribe an
application escrow window:

- [`burn-block-height`](https://docs.stacks.co/reference/clarity/keywords) returns the height of
  the underlying burn blockchain.
- [Bitcoin Finality](https://docs.stacks.co/learn/block-production/bitcoin-finality) explains that
  tenure N state is anchored at Bitcoin block N+1.
- [Pegging out sBTC](https://docs.stacks.co/learn/sbtc/sbtc-operations/withdrawal) documents a
  six-Bitcoin-confirmation rule for the distinct cross-chain withdrawal flow.

No official source reviewed here requires 144 burn blocks for an application-level escrow review
period. Nayori therefore treats its window as product policy: time for an evaluator to act before
a permissionless provider payout, not a consensus-finality parameter.

The approved next candidate uses a fixed 12-burn-block window. This preserves a margin over the
documented six-block sBTC withdrawal reference while reducing average provider capital lockup from
roughly one day to roughly two hours. Those durations are estimates because Bitcoin block times
vary. Per-job configurability is deferred to keep the external-review delta minimal.

The deployed `agentic-commerce-v3` and `sbtc-commerce-v2` sources remain unchanged at 144 blocks.
The 12-block policy is isolated in new immutable names, `agentic-commerce-v4` and
`sbtc-commerce-v3`, and remains testnet-only until review and release gates are complete.

## PoX-5 sBTC testnet migration

Stacks Labs announced the PoX-5 public testnet with a new official sBTC token:

- announcement: https://www.stacks.co/blog/the-public-pox-5-testnet-is-live-test-bitcoin-staking-before-mainnet
- token: `SN3VMHXEN64ZZF71JQ5VESXDWTR301XTTXGF4J8F1.sbtc-token`
- registry: `SN3VMHXEN64ZZF71JQ5VESXDWTR301XTTXGF4J8F1.sbtc-registry`

The authenticated Hiro Platform faucet independently confirmed the token identity. Request tx
`0x90996888f75472038047885b35775a3f6a32aa1d0648a7e9c84882d2140b7a41` succeeded at block 207783
and transferred 5,000,000 units to the dedicated Nayori testnet deployer. The older
`ST1F7QA2MDF17S807EPA36TSS8AMEFY4KA9TVGWXT.sbtc-token` constant is therefore obsolete for active
testnet flows.

Nayori will rotate only the versioned candidate's testnet payment-token configuration. M1 and
mainnet remain unchanged, and no Clarity source or deployment address changes. See
`docs/plans/2026-08-29-pox5-sbtc-testnet-migration-design.md`.
